Computer Systems Engineering student building embedded, networked and secure systems, from the firmware on a microcontroller to the server it talks to.

Biography

I'm studying for an Engineering Degree in Computer Systems Engineering at MedTech (South Mediterranean University) in Tunis (2023 to 2028). I like building things where hardware and software meet: microcontrollers that talk to web servers, devices on PCBs I designed myself, and the network code that connects them.

At 15 I built a Mario-style 2D game and took 3rd place at a GoMyCode hackathon. At 18 I ran my own small online business, MetaShop. At MedTech I've built a voice-controlled smart house on STM32, a multiplayer game over raw TCP/UDP sockets and an ESP32 Wi-Fi mapper on a custom PCB, and I spent a summer as a cybersecurity intern at Network Associates, securing a real desktop application.

Where I'm heading: I want to work on embedded and connected systems that are secure by design, from firmware up to the cloud. Outside engineering I edit videos: I co-founded Rakcha Koora, a football quiz channel, and was its lead video editor.
