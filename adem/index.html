Biography

I'm studying for an Engineering Degree in Computer Systems Engineering at MedTech – South Mediterranean University in Tunis (2023 – 2028), after finishing high school at Abu Loubaba in Gabès.

I started with web development: in high school I built an online appointment management system for medical appointments with HTML, CSS, PHP, SQL and JavaScript. At MedTech I've worked in teams on seven projects – from IoT devices like a smart plug and a waste-sorting bin, to a voice-controlled smart house on STM32, a Wi-Fi mapper on a custom PCB, a machine-learning study and a multiplayer network game.

Where I'm heading: I want to build reliable software for real users, especially web applications and data-driven tools. Outside engineering I play football in tournaments and volunteer in my community.
