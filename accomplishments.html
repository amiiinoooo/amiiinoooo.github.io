Extracurricular activities

What I've built and taken part in outside the classroom.

MetaShopFounder, Oct 2022 to Jul 2023

I started and ran MetaShop on my own: an online business selling digital gift cards (PlayStation, Apple, Steam, Epic Games and more) at affordable prices in Tunisia.

  • Managed the entire operation: sourcing, pricing, order handling, payments and customer service.
  • Processed 86+ orders for 35+ customers, about 10K TND in sales. About half of the customers came back to order again.
  • Promoted it on Instagram with a public reviews section and clear ordering guides to build trust.
  • Funded by a private investor.

What I learned: running a business taught me to own every detail, answer customers fast and keep promises. I bring the same habits to team projects now.

Rakcha KooraCo-founder and lead video editor, Feb to Aug 2024

Co-founded Rakcha Koora, a football quiz channel run by a team of four, where we recorded and played the quizzes on camera.

  • Lead video editor for all content: long-form YouTube episodes (25 to 35 min), YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok videos.
  • Managed the channel's social media across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
  • Reached 23K+ views, 400+ followers and 600+ likes across all platforms.

What I learned: editing for an audience means cutting whatever doesn't serve the story. It also made me better at presentations and demos.
