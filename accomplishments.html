Entrepreneurship

Founder – MetaShop

Oct 2022 – Jul 2023

I started and ran MetaShop on my own: a small online business selling e-cards and gift cards at low prices in Tunisia. I handled everything – sourcing, pricing, orders and customer service.

What I learned: running a business taught me to own every detail, answer customers fast and keep promises – the same habits I now bring to team projects.
Media

Co-founder & Video Editor – RakchaKoora

2023

With a group of friends I co-founded a football YouTube channel. I was the main video editor for the YouTube videos, Instagram Reels and TikToks, using Adobe Premiere Pro.

What I learned: editing for an audience is about rhythm and clarity – cut what doesn't serve the story. It made me better at presentations and demos too.
